📝 Exam Writing Strategy
⏰

15-Minute Reading Time

Use carefully to select and plan questions. Don't write anything.

✍️

LOC Format for Audit

Letter of Comment is non-negotiable. Structure: Summary → LOC → Observations.

📊

Show Full Workings

Even if final answer is wrong, step marks fetch 60-70% of total.

⚡

1.5 Min Per Mark Rule

4-hour paper × 60 marks = 240 minutes. Allocate: 15 min reading + 225 min writing.

📝

Headings & Structure

Clear headings = easy scanning by examiners. Improves chances of bonus marks.

🎯

Attempt All Questions

Even partial attempts fetch 30-40 marks. Blanks fetch zero. Always attempt.
